Notes on method:
- BB listing pages (Apacs, 4 pages; Pro Series page 1) were read for product URLs. Product pages were fetched one at a time and the values below are what the page states.
- Where a row's source is a WebSearch result snippet and not a fetched page, the notes column says "snippet only".
- Flex class: "Medium Stiff" counts as stiff. The Apacs number is kept in the flex column. "Medium Flexible" is shown as the source wrote it. A 2-D band like 9-10 is kept as written.
- Balance: the source label is used. Where a source gave only mm, the mm rule applies (300 or more head-heavy, 290 or less head-light, otherwise even) and the cell says "(by mm rule)". Several Apacs pages say "Slightly Head Heavy" at 285-295 mm; the source label is kept and noted.
- Max lbs is the figure stated; Stern 90 Offensive lists separate V/H tensions.
- Player level only where the source states it.
