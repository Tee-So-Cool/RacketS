# Victor spec research, pass 4

Date: 2026-10-28. Targets: remaining gaps in victor-gaps.txt. Models already filled in victor-specs.md, -2 and -3 are not repeated unless a new source adds a field or exposes a conflict.

Sources used: sakurasport.com (Victor's German distributor shop; it prints "Equilibre" and "Rigidite" as text: Poids en tete = head-heavy, Neutre = even, Poids en manche = head-light; Tres flexible / Flexible / Semi-rigide / Rigide), sportsshop.asia (Singapore, SGD; text plus a five-dot scale), yumo.ca (Shopify product feed; price currency not printed, probably CAD, so not used), joybadminton.com (feed; flex field is empty on most pages), badmintonwarehouse.com and nydhi.com (feeds), badmintonplaza.com listing in THB, momoshop.com.tw and pchome (Taiwan), kibisports (India). Some joybadminton, badmintonplanet.eu, yumo blog and sportano pages gave HTTP 429 or 403; those were not retried, and where a value comes only from a search-result summary of such a page this is said in notes.

Conventions: "semi-stiff / Semi-rigide / medium stiff" is written as stiff, as in earlier rounds, and noted. yumo.ca prints a five-dot scale "S o o o o o F"; it decodes as S = stiff end, F = flexible end (HMR Pro, 800HT and DriveX 1A are known flexible and the dot sits at F; Fantome F HYQ M has the dot near S and is stiff). No balance was derived from mm (the only mm value found was 298 +/- 3 mm on a 100X listing, not a gap model).
