# Li-Ning specs, round 3 (official regional stores)

Primary source: the official Li-Ning Studio Shopify stores (sg.lining.studio = Singapore/SGD, in.lining.studio = India/INR, ae.lining.studio = UAE/AED), product pages and public product feeds fetched 2026-10-28 at a polite pace. Their spec block gives balance (word), balance point (mm), shaft flexibility, weight in grams, max tension (often blank on the SG pages, filled on the IN pages) and a "player level" field, which is the manufacturer-side statement used for the level column (Professional = pro, Intermediate = intermediate). No Thai Li-Ning price source was reachable, so prices are SGD / INR / AED (JSON feed prices; the SG page "MRP incl. taxes" can be a few dollars higher). Where a page gives grams only, U class is inferred from Li-Ning's own gram bands and flagged in notes.
Not found on the three official stores under these names: Axforce 9 (only Axforce 90 exists, no row), Axforce 80 Light (sibling rows from Axforce 80 5U/4U pages given), Halbertec 7000 II (sibling Halbertec 7000), BladeX 73 Light (sibling BladeX 73), SS 100 Superlight (sibling Super Series SS 100), Axforce 90 Max Dragon (alias of Axforce 90 Dragon Max). No Thai-market Li-Ning page or lining.com page turned up in search.
