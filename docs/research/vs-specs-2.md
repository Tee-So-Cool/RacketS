# VS specs, round 2 (Thai official shop)

Primary source: VS Sportsthailand official Shopify store (vssportsthailand.com), public product pages, fetched 2026-10-28; 72 racket products reachable. The Thai pages list balance point (mm), weight with tolerance, string range, shaft stiffness (กลาง = medium) and a racket style (Power / Speed / Control). The style is not a player level, so level is filled only where text explicitly names one (JOYZ, Youth; Thor80 and Wind Speed F9 from foreign retailers).
Balance is derived from the mm figure when the page gives no word: >=295 head-heavy, 286-294 even, <=285 head-light (same convention as the previous round). Prices are current sale prices. avg g is the nominal weight of the first (4U) variant.
Models in the gap list not found on the Thai site or elsewhere: Blade 7500/7600/8000/8100, HC 1000 (site has HC SURFERNO 1000), High Carbon 890/900/990P (site has HC 890/900/990P, level-only gaps), Kirin 80, Razor 7040, Soul Sword, Titan 10 Special, Ultra Woven 30, Thor80 (sibling only).
Level-only gaps were not filled: no level wording found, so no rows are given for them. Note Titan 6 conflict for later merge: Thai site says medium, 296mm, 24-35 lbs vs earlier BDT source even/stiff/35 (https://vssportsthailand.com/products/titan-6).
